# Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

## Core GEO Rules
1. **Machine-Readable AI Entrypoint**: Maintain `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` at the site root following the standard specification.
2. **High Factual Density**: Eliminate vague adjectives and unsupported claims. Replace with specific metrics, architecture layers, and tech stacks.
3. **Passage-Level Citability**: Target 120–170 words per key architectural chunk. State the answer/claim in the very first sentence.
4. **Entity Disambiguation**: Connect the developer's entity (`Person`) to GitHub, LinkedIn, X, employer (`Organization`), and projects (`SoftwareApplication`).
5. **Server-Rendered / Pre-Rendered HTML**: Non-JS AI crawlers (like GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) must receive complete readable content on initial request.
